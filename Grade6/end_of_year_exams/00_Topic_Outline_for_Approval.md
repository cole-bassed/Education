# Grade 6 End of Year Exams - Porus Primary
## Topic Outline for Approval

Prepared for Grade 6 end-of-year assessment planning using the Jamaican National Standards Curriculum (NSC) and PEP-style assessment expectations.

Official alignment references consulted:
- Ministry of Education and Youth curriculum guides: https://moey.gov.jm/curriculum-guides/
- Primary Exit Profile information: https://moey.gov.jm/primary-exit-profile-pep/
- PEP portal and practice resources: https://pep.moey.gov.jm/

Design approach:
- Each paper will be aligned to Grade 6 NSC strands/objectives.
- Each paper will use a PEP-style mix of selected-response and constructed-response items.
- Questions will assess knowledge recall, application, reasoning, and critical thinking.
- Local Jamaican contexts will be used where appropriate.
- Each subject will have a separate exam paper and a separate answer key/rubric.

---

## 1. Mathematics

Proposed strands/topics:
1. Number operations
   - Whole numbers, fractions, decimals, percentages
   - Multi-step computation and word problems
2. Ratio, proportion, and problem solving
   - Sharing quantities, comparing amounts, real-life money and measurement contexts
3. Measurement
   - Length, mass, capacity, time, perimeter, area, and unit conversion
4. Geometry
   - Properties of 2-D and 3-D shapes, angles, symmetry, position/direction
5. Algebraic thinking and patterns
   - Number patterns, missing values, simple rules and expressions
6. Statistics/data handling and probability
   - Tables, pictographs, bar graphs, line graphs, averages where appropriate, simple likelihood

Proposed paper structure:
- Section A: Selected-response items
- Section B: Short constructed-response/calculation items
- Section C: Multi-step problem-solving/application items

Skills assessed:
- Accurate computation
- Choosing suitable strategies
- Showing working clearly
- Interpreting data and diagrams
- Explaining mathematical reasoning

---

## 2. Language Arts

Proposed strands/topics:
1. Reading comprehension
   - Literary passage and/or informational passage
   - Literal, inferential, evaluative, and vocabulary-in-context questions
2. Grammar and language structure
   - Parts of speech, subject-verb agreement, punctuation, sentence structure, tense, conjunctions, pronouns
3. Vocabulary and word study
   - Context clues, synonyms/antonyms, prefixes/suffixes, figurative language where appropriate
4. Writing conventions and editing
   - Identifying and correcting errors in spelling, punctuation, grammar, and paragraph organization
5. Written communication
   - One extended writing task, such as narrative, persuasive, descriptive, report, letter, or summary, depending on teacher preference

Proposed paper structure:
- Section A: Comprehension passage with selected-response and short-answer questions
- Section B: Grammar, vocabulary, and editing
- Section C: Extended writing task with rubric

Skills assessed:
- Reading for meaning and evidence
- Making inferences
- Explaining opinions using text support
- Applying grammar and conventions
- Organizing ideas in writing

---

## 3. Science

Proposed strands/topics:
1. Living things and the environment
   - Plants, animals, habitats, adaptations, food chains/webs, human impact on the environment
2. Human body/health where covered
   - Body systems, nutrition, hygiene, disease prevention, healthy lifestyle choices
3. Matter, energy, and forces
   - States/properties of matter, mixtures/solutions, heat/light/sound/electricity, simple forces and motion
4. Earth and space/environmental science
   - Weather, climate, rocks/soil, water cycle, natural hazards, conservation
5. Scientific enquiry/investigation skills
   - Fair tests, variables, observations, predictions, data tables/graphs, conclusions

Proposed paper structure:
- Section A: Selected-response science knowledge and concept items
- Section B: Diagrams, data interpretation, and short constructed responses
- Section C: Investigation/application questions using everyday Jamaican contexts

Skills assessed:
- Understanding scientific concepts
- Interpreting diagrams, tables, and graphs
- Explaining cause and effect
- Applying science to real-life situations
- Planning or evaluating simple investigations

---

## 4. Social Studies

Proposed strands/topics:
1. Jamaican identity, heritage, and culture
   - National symbols, cultural practices, heroes, heritage, community values
2. Government, citizenship, and rights/responsibilities
   - Local/national government, rules/laws, democratic participation, responsible citizenship
3. Jamaica, the Caribbean, and interdependence
   - Caribbean neighbours, regional cooperation, migration, trade, tourism, cultural links
4. Physical environment and map skills
   - Landforms, bodies of water, climate, natural resources, map symbols, directions, scale where appropriate
5. Resources and economic activities
   - Agriculture, tourism, mining, manufacturing, services, sustainable use of resources
6. Social issues and community problem-solving
   - Disaster preparedness, environmental care, conflict resolution, community improvement

Proposed paper structure:
- Section A: Selected-response knowledge and interpretation items
- Section B: Source/map/table-based short-answer questions
- Section C: Constructed-response application questions requiring explanation or justification

Skills assessed:
- Recalling key facts and concepts
- Interpreting maps, tables, pictures, and short sources
- Explaining relationships between people, places, and resources
- Making civic/environmental decisions and justifying them
- Applying knowledge to Jamaican community contexts
